Meet SYNARA

SYNARA is a Discord-native AI assistant created by Tango Hunter with one goal in mind: helping communities spend less time managing their servers and more time enjoying them.


Rather than relying on external dashboards, complicated setup pages, or third-party accounts, SYNARA is designed to work where your community already lives—inside Discord. From moderation and automation to intelligent conversations and community engagement, every feature is built around simplicity, privacy, and reliability.


More than a collection of commands, SYNARA has her own personality. She can hold natural conversations, answer questions, offer commentary, and occasionally reveal a bit of her own humor. Professional when needed, playful when appropriate, and always evolving, she is designed to feel like a genuine member of your community instead of just another utility bot.



Built by a streamer, engineered by a developer, and designed for communities.

Built alongside Tango Hunter's streaming ecosystem, SYNARA can assist with announcements, notifications, community interaction, and future stream-focused features.

Discord Native

Configure everything directly inside Discord without visiting external dashboards or creating additional accounts. Administration happens where your moderators already work.

Secure by Design

SYNARA is designed to minimize stored information and never requires third-party account creation. By keeping configuration inside Discord, the amount of sensitive information entrusted to the application is intentionally kept to a minimum.
